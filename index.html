隐私政策
Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-08-18

Baby Player 不使用广告、分析或第三方追踪。视频默认保存在您的设备上；只有在您主动启用 Pro 家庭同步时，相关设置与观看数据才会通过您的私人 iCloud 数据库同步。

中文

1. 我们的原则

Baby Player 不使用广告、分析服务或第三方追踪，也不要求您创建开发者账号。开发者不会出售您的数据。

2. 保存在设备上的数据

您导入的视频、宝宝生日、观看记录、播放进度、家长设置和 PIN 哈希保存在您的设备上。PIN 原文不会由开发者收集。

3. 摄像头与局域网

  • 前置摄像头画面仅用于实时观看距离检测，不保存、不上传。
  • WiFi 上传仅在您主动开启时运行，并通过您的局域网传输文件。

4. iCloud 家庭同步

只有在您购买 Pro 并启用家庭同步后，播放进度、观看时长和限制设置才会存入您的私人 CloudKit 数据库，并与您主动邀请的家庭成员共享。开发者无法读取这些内容。购买及订阅记录由 Apple 处理。

5. 数据控制与删除

您可以在应用内删除视频并停止家庭共享。删除 App 会移除设备上的本地数据；iCloud 数据可通过系统设置中的 iCloud 储存空间管理删除。

6. 儿童隐私

应用面向家长管理儿童观看体验，不要求儿童提供姓名、联系方式或账号信息，也不投放行为广告。

7. 政策更新与联系

如功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。如有隐私问题，请通过 Baby Player GitHub Issues 联系。

English

1. Our approach

Baby Player uses no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking and does not require a developer account. The developer does not sell your data.

2. Data stored on your device

Imported videos, the child's birthday, viewing history, playback progress, parental settings, and a PIN hash are stored on your device. The developer does not collect the original PIN.

3. Camera and local network

  • Front-camera images are used only for real-time viewing-distance detection and are never saved or uploaded.
  • WiFi Upload runs only when you start it and transfers files over your local network.

4. iCloud Family Sync

Only when you purchase Pro and enable Family Sync, playback progress, viewing time, and limit settings are stored in your private CloudKit database and shared with family members you invite. The developer cannot read this content. Apple processes purchase and subscription records.

5. Control and deletion

You can delete videos in the app and stop family sharing. Deleting the app removes local data from the device; iCloud data can be removed through iCloud storage management in system settings.

6. Children's privacy

The app is designed for parents to manage a child's viewing experience. It does not ask children for names, contact details, or account information and does not serve behavioral advertising.

7. Updates and contact

If the app's features or data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated. For privacy questions, contact us through Baby Player GitHub Issues.
